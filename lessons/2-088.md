---
title: Trapped in a mine
titleCn: 困在矿井里
---

## English

Six men have been trapped in a mine for seventeen hours. If they are not brought to the surface soon they may lose their lives. However, rescue operations are proving difficult. A tunnel has to be dug through hard rock. The drill is very powerful but progress is slow. Rescue workers have tried to reach the men with a capsule lowered down a narrow opening. They have been working for several hours, but the capsule has not been able to get far. The men have been sending up signals from the mine. They have been running out of food and drink. The rescue workers are drilling a hole through the rock to reach the trapped miners. When the hole has been completed, the men will be able to enter the tunnel. They will then be able to reach the trapped miners and bring them up to the surface.

## 中文

6个人被困在矿井里已有17个小时了。如果不把他们尽快救到地面上来，他们就有可能丧生。然而，事实证明营救工作非常困难。必须钻透坚硬的岩石挖一条隧道。钻机虽然很得力，但进度缓慢。营救人员曾试图用一种容器从一个窄小的开口处放下绳子去接近他们。他们干了好几个小时，但容器未能深入很远。被困人员从矿井里不断发出信号。他们的食物和饮料都快用完了。营救人员正在钻透岩石以接近被困矿工。当洞钻通后，人们将能进入隧道，然后就能接近被困的矿工并把他们救上地面。
