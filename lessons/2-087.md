---
title: A perfect alibi
titleCn: 极好的不在犯罪现场的证据
---

## English

'At the time the murder was committed, I was travelling on the 8 o'clock train to London,' said the man.

'Do you always catch such an early train?' asked the inspector.

'Of course I do,' answered the man. 'I must be at work at 10 o'clock. My employer would confirm that I was there on time.'

'Would a later train get you to work on time?' asked the inspector.

'I suppose it would, but I never catch a later train.'

'At what time did you arrive at the station?'

'At ten to eight. I bought a paper and waited for the train.'

'And you didn't notice anything unusual?'

'Of course not.'

'I suggest,' said the inspector, 'that you are not telling the truth. I suggest that you did not catch the 8 o'clock train, but that you caught the 8.25 which would still get you to work on time. You see, on the morning of the murder, the 8 o'clock train did not run at all. It broke down at Ferngreen station and was taken off the line.'

## 中文

"凶杀发生的时候，我正坐在8点钟开往伦敦的火车上，"那人说。

"您总是赶这样早的火车吗？"探长问。

"当然是的，"那人回答，"我必须在10点钟上班，我的雇主会证明我是按时到了的。"

"晚一点儿的车也能送您按时上班吗？"

"我认为可以，但我从来不坐晚一点儿的车。"

"您几点到的车站？"

"7点50分。我买了张报纸，等着车。"

"您没有注意到有什么异常情况吗？"

"当然没有。"

"我提醒您，"探长说，"您讲的不是实话。您没有坐8点钟的火车，而是坐的8点25分的火车，这班车也能使您按时上班。您看，在凶杀发生的那天早晨，8点钟的那趟车根本没有运行，它在芬格林车站出了故障，被取消了。"
