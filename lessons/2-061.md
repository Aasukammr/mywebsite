---
title: Trouble with the Hubble
titleCn: 哈勃望远镜的困境
---

## English

The Hubble telescope was launched into space on April 20, 1990 at a cost of over a billion dollars. Right from the start there was trouble with it. The pictures it sent us were very disappointing because its main mirror was faulty! NASA is now going to put the telescope right, so it will soon be sending up four astronauts to repair it. The repair work will be carried out by the Space Shuttle Endeavour. The astronauts will make a number of necessary repairs. Of course, the shuttle will be taking the workers and the equipment to the Hubble. A robot-arm from the Endeavour will grab the telescope and hold it while the astronauts make the repairs. By the time the Endeavour returns to Earth, the telescope will have been functioning perfectly. Great excitement has been caused by the forthcoming repairs. When the Hubble was launched, there was great interest. The Hubble will send us the most wonderful photographs of our galaxy and will give us a clear picture of how the universe began.

## 中文

哈勃望远镜于1990年4月20日发射升空，耗资10多亿美元。从最开始哈勃望远镜就有问题。它传送给我们的图像很令人失望，因为它的主镜片有误差。国家航天局准备把望远镜修好，他们将送去4名宇航员来修复。修复工作将由"奋进"号航天飞机来实施。宇航员将进行几次必要的修复。当然，航天飞机将把工人和设备带给哈勃。当宇航员进行修复时，"奋进"号上的机器人手臂将抓住望远镜并把它固定住。当"奋进"号返回地球时，望远镜将已经能正常工作了。即将进行的修复工作引起了巨大的激动。哈勃望远镜发射时，人们对此有极大的兴趣。哈勃将向我们提供我们银河系最精彩的照片，并将告诉我们宇宙是怎样形成的。
