## English

At a village fair, I decided to visit a fortune-teller called Madam Bellinsky. I went into her tent and she told me to sit down. After I had stared into a crystal ball, she said that a relation of mine was coming to see me. She added that this relation would be arriving in a few minutes and that after he left I would follow him. My relation himself turned up at the door exactly as she was speaking. My aunt was accompanied by her daughter Jane. I had not seen my aunt for years. I knew that my aunt was coming to see me. But not only did she appear, but she brought her daughter with her. I was very pleased to see them, but I have to admit that I was also a little disappointed. As I had expected, my aunt was not carrying a message from a fairy, but it was a letter.

## 中文

在一个乡村集市上，我决定去拜访一位叫别林斯基夫人的算命人。我走进她的帐篷，她叫我坐下。她盯着水晶球，告诉我，我的一个亲戚要来看我。她补充说，这位亲戚几分钟后就到，而且他走之后，我也会跟着去。她正说着，我的一位亲戚就在门口出现了。我的姑妈是和她的女儿简一起来的。我已经好多年没有见过我的姑妈了。我知道姑妈要来看我，但令我吃惊的是不仅她来了，还带来了女儿。看见他们，我很高兴，但又不得不承认也有些失望。正如我所料，姑妈带来的不是仙女的信，而是一封信。
