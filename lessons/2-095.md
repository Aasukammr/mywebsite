## English

When the Ambassador of Escalopia returned home for lunch, his wife got a shock. He looked pale and his clothes were in a frightful state.

'What has happened?' she asked. 'How did your clothes get into such a mess?'

'Fire extinguisher, my dear,' the Ambassador answered calmly. 'University students set the Embassy on fire this morning.'

'Good heavens!' she exclaimed. 'And where were you at the time?'

'I was in my room,' the Ambassador answered. 'I was having a bath when the fire started. I managed to get hold of the extinguisher and put the fire out. Unfortunately, a fireman came in and aimed a jet of water at me. It soaked me from head to foot. As you can imagine, I'm not very happy about it. The fireman seemed to think that I was on fire.'

The Ambassador's wife took a deep breath. She looked at the Ambassador's hat. It was not a hat at all. It was a fire extinguisher. It had been a perfect fit.

## 中文

当艾斯卡罗比亚国的大使回到家吃午饭时，他的夫人吃了一惊。他面色苍白，衣服也搞得一塌糊涂。

"发生了什么事？"她问，"你的衣服怎么搞得这么糟？"

"灭火器，亲爱的，"大使冷漠地回答，"今天上午大学生们放火烧了大使馆。"

"天啊！"夫人惊叫，"那你当时在什么地方？"

"当时我在房间里，大使回答说，"火灾发生时我正在洗澡。我设法找到了灭火器，把火扑灭了。不幸的是，一个消防队员闯了进来，拿水龙头对着我浇了一身，把我从头到脚都浇透了。你可以想象，我对此非常不快。那个消防队员好像以为我着火了。"

大使夫人深吸了一口气。她看了看大使的帽子。那根本就不像一顶帽子，那是一只灭火器。它戴在头上倒挺合适。
