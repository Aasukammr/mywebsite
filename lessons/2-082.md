---
title: Monster or fish?
titleCn: 是妖还是鱼？
---

## English

Fishermen and sailors sometimes claim to have seen monsters in the sea. Though people have often laughed at stories told by seamen, it is now known that many of these 'monsters' which have at times been sighted are simply strange fish. Occasionally, unusual creatures are washed to the shore, but they are rarely caught out at sea. Some time ago, however, a peculiar fish was caught near Madagascar. A small fishing boat was carried miles out to sea by the powerful fish as it pulled on the line. Realizing that this was no ordinary fish, the fisherman made every effort not to damage it in any way. When it was eventually brought to shore, it was found to be over thirteen feet long. It had a head like a horse, big blue eyes, and a long tail. The fish, which has since been sent to a museum where it is being examined by a scientist, is called an oarfish. Such creatures have rarely been seen alive by man as they live at a depth of six hundred feet.

## 中文

渔夫和水手们有时声称自己看到过海里的妖怪。虽然人们常常对水手们讲的故事付诸一笑，但现在看来，人们有时看到的这些"妖怪"很多不过是奇怪的鱼罢了。一些异常的生物偶尔会被冲到岸边，但很少会在海里被捕到。然而不久前，在马达加斯加附近捕到了一条怪鱼。一条小渔船被一条非常强壮的鱼拖到了几英里外的海面上。那位渔民意识到这根本不是一条普通的鱼，于是千方百计不让它受到丝毫伤害。当终于把它弄上岸后，发现它身长超过了13英尺。它长着一个像马一样的头，有着大大的蓝眼睛和一条长长的尾巴。此鱼叫桨鱼，被送进了博物馆，现正接受一位科学家的检查。人们极少看到活着的这类动物，因为它们生活在600英尺深的水下。
