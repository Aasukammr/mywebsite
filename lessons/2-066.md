## English

In 1963 a Lancaster bomber crashed on a remote part of the island of New Britain in the South Pacific. The plane wasn't too badly damaged, but over the years, the crash was forgotten and the wreck remained undisturbed. Then in 1971, a newspaper organised an expedition to look for the bomber and a journalist was the only person to find it. By this time, a Lancaster bomber in reasonable condition was rare and worth rescuing. The French authorities had the plane packaged and moved in parts back to France. Now a group of enthusiasts are going to have the plane restored. It has four Rolls-Royce Merlin engines, but the group will need to have only three of them rebuilt. Imagine their surprise and delight when they broke open the packing cases and found that the fourth engine was sweet as honey — still in perfect condition. A colony of bees had turned the engine into a hive and it was totally preserved in beeswax!

## 中文

1963年，一架兰开斯特轰炸机在远离南太平洋的新不列颠岛的一个偏远地区坠毁。飞机损坏的程度并不严重，但多年来这起飞机失事已被遗忘，飞机残骸也没受到破坏。1971年，一家报纸发起了一次寻找这架轰炸机的远征考察，只有一位记者找到了飞机。此时，状况良好的兰开斯特轰炸机实属罕见，值得抢救。法国政府让人把飞机包装起来，一部分一部分地运回法国。现在一群热心人计划修复这架飞机。该机装配有4台罗尔斯-罗伊斯默林发动机，但他们只需要修复其中的3台。想一想他们所感受到的惊奇和兴奋——当他们打开包装箱时，发现第4台发动机像蜂蜜一样甜——发动机完好无损。一群蜜蜂把发动机当成了蜂房，发动机在蜂蜡中被完整地保存了下来！
