## English

I used to travel by air a great deal when I was a boy. My parents used to live in South America and I used to fly there from Europe in the holidays. An air-hostess would take charge of me and I never had an unpleasant experience. I am used to travelling by air and only on one occasion have I ever felt frightened. After taking off, we were flying low over the city and it was slowly turning in the night. There was a blue sky above us and there was a bright orange glow over the city. We could see the lights of the city for a long time. The plane was very comfortable and the flight was smooth. Then we flew over the sea. The plane began to descend and the lights of another city could be seen. The pilot, who was a very experienced pilot, had made many flights across the Atlantic. He knew the route very well. It was a wonderful and exciting experience.

## 中文

我在幼年的时候，曾多次乘飞机旅行。我的父母曾住在南美洲，所以假期里我常从欧洲乘飞机到他们那里。总是由一位空中乘务员照管我，我从未有过不愉快的经历。我习惯了乘飞机旅行，只有一次把我吓坏了。起飞之后，我们在城市上空低低地飞行，城市在夜色中慢慢地在下面旋转。我们头顶上是蓝色的天空，城市上空有一抹明亮的橘红色光芒。城市里的灯光很长时间都能看见。飞机非常舒适，飞行很平稳。然后我们飞过了大海。飞机开始下降，又一座城市的灯光映入眼帘。那位飞行员经验非常丰富，他曾多次飞越大西洋。他对航线非常熟悉。那真是一次奇妙而激动人心的经历。
