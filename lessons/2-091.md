---
title: Three men in a basket
titleCn: 三人同篮
---

## English

A pilot noticed a balloon which seemed to be making for a Royal Air Force Station nearby. He reported the matter to the station and the station commander ordered that the basket containing the three men should be investigated. The basket was attached to a huge balloon. The pilot circled the balloon several times and then flew away. When the balloon landed, the men were taken to the station. The commander asked them if they had seen anything of the station. They said they had not seen anything at all. They told him that they were on a special mission. They had been flying for several hours. They were very cold and hungry. They had been in the air for more than fourteen hours. They had only had enough food and water for about eight hours. The commander was very kind to them and gave them food and drink. They thanked him and said they were very grateful.

## 中文

一个飞行员注意到一只气球好像正飞向附近的皇家空军基地。他把此事报告了基地，基地司令官下令调查一下吊篮里的三个人。吊篮挂在一只巨大的气球上。飞行员绕气球飞了几圈，然后飞走了。气球着陆后，三个人被带到了基地。司令官问他们是否看到了基地的什么东西。他们说根本什么也没看见。他们告诉司令官说他们在执行一项特殊的任务。他们已经飞了好几个小时，又冷又饿。他们已经飞行了14个多小时，而他们只带了够8个小时的食物和水。司令官对他们非常友善，给了他们食物和水。他们表示感谢，并说他们非常感激。
