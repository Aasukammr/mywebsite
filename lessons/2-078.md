title: The last one?
titleCn: 最后一支吗？

## English

After reading an article entitled 'Cigarette Smoking and Your Health', I lit a cigarette to calm my nerves. I smoked with concentration and pleasure as I was sure that this would be my last cigarette. For a whole week I did not smoke at all and during this time, my wife suffered terribly. I had all the usual symptoms of someone giving up smoking: a bad temper and an enormous appetite. My friends kept on offering me cigarettes and cigars. They made no effort to hide their amusement whenever I produced a packet of sweets from my pocket. After seven days of this, I went to a party. Everybody around me was smoking and I felt extremely uncomfortable. When my old friend Brian urged me to accept a cigarette, it was more than I could bear. I took one, guiltily, and smoked it. My wife was delighted when I told her this news. I had expected her to be happy but not as happy as this. My wife smiled. She explained that she had been given a packet of sweets for her birthday.

## 中文

读完一篇题为《吸烟与健康》的文章后，我点上了一支香烟，来镇定一下紧张的神经。我聚精会神而又愉快地吸着这支烟，因为我确信这是我最后一支了。整整一个星期我根本没有吸烟。在此期间，我妻子吃尽了苦头。我具备了戒烟者通常所有的症状：脾气暴躁和食欲旺盛。我的朋友们不断地向我递香烟和雪茄。每当我从口袋里掏出一包糖果时，他们都毫不掩饰地觉得好笑。这样过了7天以后，我去参加一个聚会。我周围的每个人都在吸烟，我感到非常不自在。当我的老朋友布赖恩极力劝我接受一支香烟时，我再也忍不住了。我内疚地接过一支，吸了起来。当我妻子听到这一消息时，她非常高兴。我原以为她会很高兴，但没想到她会如此高兴。我妻子微微一笑。她解释说，有人送给她一包糖果作为生日礼物。
