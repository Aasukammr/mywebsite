---
title: Shopping made easy
titleCn: 购物变得很方便
---

## English

People are not so honest as they once were. The temptation to steal is greater than ever before — especially in large shops. A detective recently watched a well-dressed woman who always went into a large store on Monday mornings. One Monday, there were fewer people in the shop than usual when the woman came in, so it was easier for the detective to watch her. The woman first bought a few small articles. After a little time, she chose one of the most expensive dresses in the shop and handed it to an assistant who wrapped it up for her as quickly as possible. Then the woman simply took the parcel and walked out of the shop without paying. When she was arrested, the detective found out that the shop assistant was her daughter.

## 中文

人们不再像以前那样诚实了。偷窃的诱惑力比以往任何时候都要大，尤其是在大商店里。一名侦探最近注意上了一位穿着讲究的女士，她总是在星期一上午进入一家大商店。有一个星期一，当这位女士走进商店时，店里的人比往常少，因此侦探比较容易监视她。这位女士先买了几样小商品。过了一会儿，她选了店里最昂贵的一件衣服，把它递给了售货员。那售货员以最快的速度为她包好了衣服。然后，那女士拿着包裹就走出了商店，根本没有付钱。当她被捕后，侦探发现原来那售货员是她的女儿。
