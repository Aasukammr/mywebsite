---
title: The Channel Tunnel
titleCn: 海峡隧道
---

## English

In 1858, a French engineer, Aime Thome de Gamond, arrived in England with a plan for a twenty-one-mile tunnel across the English Channel. He said that it would be possible to build a platform in the centre of the Channel. This platform would serve as a port and a railway station. The tunnel would be well-ventilated if tall chimneys were built above sea level. In 1860, a better plan was put forward by an Englishman, William Low. He suggested that a double railway tunnel should be built. This would solve the problem of ventilation, for if a train entered the tunnel, it would draw in fresh air behind it. Forty-two years later, a tunnel was actually begun. If, at the time, the British had not feared invasion, it would have been completed. It was officially opened on March 7, 1994, finally connecting Britain to the European continent.

## 中文

1858年，一位名叫埃梅·托梅·德·干蒙的法国工程师带着建造一条长21英里、穿越英吉利海峡的隧道计划到了英国。他说，可以在海峡中央建造一座平台，这座平台将用作码头和火车站。如果再建一些高大的烟囱伸出海面，就解决了隧道的通风问题。1860年，一位名叫威廉·洛的英国人提出了一项更好的计划。他提议修建一条双轨隧道，这样就解决了通风问题。42年后，隧道实际已经开工。如果不是因为当时英国人害怕入侵，隧道早就建成了。它于1994年3月7日正式开通，将英国与欧洲大陆连在了一起。
